# Discovery Document - traditiontag

## Client Requirements Summary

### Brand Identity

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Brand Name** | traditiontag |
| **Logo Asset** | `Elegant handcrafted brand design.png` (located in Downloads folder) |
| **Brand Personality** | Rich-traditional, luxury + affordable tiers |
| **Target Audience** | Indian + International customers, age 30-50 |

---

### Product Catalog

**Categories:**
- Handicraft Textiles (sarees, dupattas, stoles, home textiles)
- Home Decor (wall art, sculptures, decorative items)
- Jewelry (traditional, contemporary, tribal)
- Art (paintings, folk art, modern interpretations)
- Pottery (terracotta, blue pottery, ceramic art)

**Exclusions:**
- NO food items (no edible products)

---

### Key Features

#### 1. AI Room Visualization
- Users can upload a photo of their room
- AI places selected products in the room context
- Helps visualize scale, color harmony, and placement
- Essential for home decor, art, and furniture items

#### 2. AR Product Projection
- Mobile AR feature for real-world product preview
- Uses device camera to project 3D product models
- Accurate sizing and spatial awareness
- Primary use case: jewelry try-on, decor placement

---

### User Demographics Considerations

**Age 30-50 Design Implications:**
- Larger, clear typography (minimum 16px body)
- High contrast ratios for readability (WCAG AA minimum)
- Intuitive navigation with clear labels
- Reduced cognitive load - avoid overly trendy patterns
- Trust signals prominently displayed (reviews, certifications)
- Streamlined checkout with minimal steps

---

### Technical Requirements

| Feature | Implementation Notes |
|---------|---------------------|
| **AI Visualization** | Backend ML service for image processing + frontend upload UI |
| **AR Projection** | WebXR or native mobile AR (ARKit/ARCore) |
| **Responsive Design** | Mobile-first, but optimize for desktop (primary purchase device for 30-50 demographic) |
| **Performance** | Fast load times critical for international audience |

---

### Success Metrics

- Conversion rate improvement with AR/AI features
- Reduced return rates (better product visualization)
- Average order value increase
- Time-on-site engagement with visualization tools

---

### Assumptions & Constraints

- Logo file available in PNG format with transparency
- Product photography will be high-quality (required for AI/AR)
- 3D models needed for AR products
- Multi-currency support for international audience
- Multi-language consideration (English primary, Hindi secondary)
